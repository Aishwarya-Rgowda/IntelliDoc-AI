FREE · PRIVATE · IN YOUR BROWSER

File converter

Convert PDFs and images without uploading your files to a server.

⇄ LOCAL CONVERSION
▤

PDF to Word

Preserve each PDF page, photos and layout as a clear image in Word, with extracted editable text included afterward. Scanned PDFs are preserved visually, but their text is not OCR-editable.

Choose a PDF, then click Convert & Download.
▧

PDF to JPG

Turn every page of a PDF into a high-quality JPG image. Each page is downloaded separately.

Choose a PDF, then convert all pages into one ZIP.
▣

JPG / PNG to PDF

Combine selected JPG and PNG images into one downloadable PDF file.

Choose one or more images, then create your PDF.
✧

JPG ↔ PNG

Change image format using your browser. Choose JPG or PNG as the output format.

Choose an image and output format, then convert.
🔒 Your privacy matters

Conversion happens locally in your browser. Files are not sent to IntelliDoc or stored on a server. PDF-to-Word extracts text and creates an editable document; it does not recreate the exact original formatting. Password-protected and scanned PDFs may not work.
